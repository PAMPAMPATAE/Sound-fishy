🐟 How to Play Sounds Fishy!

1. Secret Roles: Each round has 1 Guesser, 1 Blue Fish (who gets the true answer in Thai & English), and Red Herrings (who bluff fake answers).
2. Out-Loud Answers & Elimination: Everyone except the Guesser says their answer out loud. The Guesser flips suspect cards one by one to eliminate fakes (+1 pt per fake caught).
3. Bank or Push Your Luck: Tap Bank anytime to lock in your streak points, or keep flipping—if you hit the Blue Fish, you lose your streak for that round and the Blue Fish scores +2 pts!
4. End-of-Round Reality Check: Every round ends with the real backstory revealed so you can compare it with the Blue Fish's explanation and vote on how well they did!
